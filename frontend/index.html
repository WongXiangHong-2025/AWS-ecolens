FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page, add your packaging and generate your report. There is nothing to sign up for.

What should I upload?

A clear, well-lit photo of the front of your current packaging works best. You can also upload a PDF.

What happens to my data?

EcoLens doesn't save your uploads. Your photo and details are sent to Amazon Bedrock only to write your report, and your report and chat stay in this browser tab. They're gone when you close it.

How accurate is the report?

EcoLens shows how sure it is: findings are labelled as observed, inferred, verified or unknown. Always confirm materials, costs and environmental claims with your supplier before you change your packaging.

Can I use Bahasa Malaysia?

Yes. Switch the site language at the top, and answer in English, Bahasa Malaysia or a mix. EcoLens replies in the language you mainly use.

Are the redesign images final artwork?

No. They are AI concepts to help you picture each option. Your designer and supplier should produce the final artwork.
